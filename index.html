Lily's Creator Assistant

A private tool used by its owner to manage her own TikTok creator account. It displays analytics for her own videos and helps her read and reply to messages from her followers. Every reply is reviewed and approved by a human before it is sent.

This application is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone other than the account owner and the messages followers send to her account.
