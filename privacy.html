Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

This policy describes how Lily's Creator Assistant ("the Service") handles data. The Service is a private tool used by its owner to manage her own TikTok account.

1. Data we access

Through TikTok's official APIs and with the account owner's authorization, the Service accesses: the owner's profile information and account statistics, the owner's own video data and performance metrics, and direct messages sent to the owner's account by her followers.

2. How data is used

Data is used only to display analytics to the account owner and to help her draft replies to messages she has received. Message content may be processed by an AI service (Anthropic) solely to generate a suggested reply for the owner's review. Data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

3. Data storage and retention

The Service runs on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally. No follower data is retained beyond what is needed to display and respond to conversations.

4. Data deletion

Followers can delete their messages through TikTok at any time. To request deletion of any data handled by the Service, contact the email below.

5. Third-party services

The Service uses TikTok APIs (governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy) and the Anthropic API (governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy).
